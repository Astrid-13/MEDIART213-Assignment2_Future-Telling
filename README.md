USER GUIDE - How to Navigate the Site

About This Experience:
Welcome to a future vision of a world where people live inside their own dreams and virtual simulations. This website takes you on a journey through three increasingly deep dream layers. As you move further into this world, the boundary between dreams and reality gradually becomes harder to recognize.
Follow the steps below to explore the experience.

Step 1 — Enter the Dream
Start by opening index.html.
Audio note: Some browsers (including Chrome), when opening the website directly from local files, may block audio playback or audio processing. For the best experience on a MacBook, use Safari, or open the project through Live Server in VS Code. Make sure your browser allows audio playback.

Step 2 — Explore Each Dream Layer
Once you enter a dream layer, take some time to explore its visual and sound elements.
•	Wake Up: You can find the WAKE UP option in the upper-right corner of each layer. However, as you go deeper, it gradually becomes harder to notice and recognize.
•	The Spinning Totem: Watch the spinning top. It stops spinning sooner in each deeper layer, suggesting that this world is becoming increasingly unstable and less real.
•	The Clock: Pay attention to the clock. Time moves faster as you descend through the layers, reflecting the growing distortion of your sense of reality.

Step 3 — Explore the Emotion Areas
At the lower part of each layer, you will find different emotion areas.
Move your mouse over each area to activate its corresponding soundtrack and explore its visual effects. You can also notice how each emotion area subtly moves and responds to your mouse.
As you descend, the same images become increasingly blurred, while the corresponding music becomes more muffled. The emotions may seem familiar, but their representations become more distorted with each layer.

Step 4 — Descend Deeper
When you are ready, click the button at the bottom of the page to move into the next dream layer. The transition takes you further downward, creating the feeling of continuously sinking deeper into the dream.
The deeper you go, the less stable and recognizable the world becomes.

Step 5 — Try to Wake Up in Layer 3
By the third layer, waking up is no longer easy. Try clicking the WAKE UP button, but be prepared: it may be difficult to reach. Keep trying if you want to return to reality.
EasterEgg — Explore the Bottom of Layer 3
Before leaving, take a closer look around the bottom area of the third layer. Try exploring beyond the obvious navigation options. You may discover something unexpected — perhaps a surprise, or perhaps something unsettling.
Your choices lead to two different possible endings.

Enjoy the experience, and remember to question what is real ~
